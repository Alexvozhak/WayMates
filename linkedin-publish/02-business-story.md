# Pathfinders who made it. Waymates who want the same.

There comes a moment when you're at a crossroads. You don't know where to go.

I wanted a personalized solution:
- See those who WERE in my situation — what their decisions led to
- Find those who are ALSO at a crossroads — walk toward the goal together

Not generic advice. Real roadmaps of people from my context.

Didn't find one. Built it.

---

**What problems does WayMates solve?**

**Got laid off?** You need a plan, not motivational posts. WayMates shows people who've been through this — their steps, timeline, what worked.

**Want to relocate?** Find those already in Amsterdam/Berlin/London with your background. Their visa journey, pitfalls, real timelines.

**Student with no direction?** See trajectories of graduates from your field. Not career center advice, but real paths of real people.

**Not ready to transition yet?** Cast a line — follow a direction you're interested in. When relevant roadmaps appear, you'll be the first to know.

---

**How it works**

Tell your story — full history or brief. The platform finds:

- **Pathfinders** — who WERE in your context and ACHIEVED your goal
- **Waymates** — who ARE in your context heading toward the same goal

Get trajectory visualization and personalized recommendations. No coaches. No paid subscriptions to people. 100% automated, works 24/7.

---

**What's planned**

**Mutual Connect** — found your pathfinder? Send a request. If they're interested too — exchange contacts. Like dating apps, but for careers. No spam.

**Privacy Tiers** — the more data you give, the less you pay. Companies see only aggregates, never individual stories.
